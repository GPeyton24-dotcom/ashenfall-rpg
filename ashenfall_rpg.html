Quick rules

Choose story options to explore. In battle, select an action each round. Potions restore health. Defeating enemies grants XP; each level improves your hero and unlocks stronger combat. Companion affinity rises when you choose thoughtful or supportive responses. Romance is optional and develops through story choices.
